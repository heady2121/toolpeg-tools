About this tool

Combine multiple PDFs — chapters, scanned pages, signed forms — into a single document, in whatever order you choose.

How it works

  1. Drop two or more PDF files.
  2. Reorder them with the up/down arrows.
  3. Merge and download the combined PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit to how many PDFs I can merge?

No fixed limit — it depends on your device's memory for very large or numerous files.

Does merging preserve the original quality?

Yes — pages are copied directly rather than re-rendered, so there's no quality loss.
