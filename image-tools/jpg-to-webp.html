About this tool

WebP typically produces smaller files than JPG at the same visual quality, which is why most modern websites use it to speed up page loads. This tool re-encodes your JPG as WebP directly in your browser.

How it works

  1. Drop or select a JPG file.
  2. Your browser decodes and re-encodes it as WebP.
  3. Download the converted file.

Frequently asked questions

Will every browser open the WebP file?

All current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari display WebP images natively. Very old browsers may not.

Is WebP always smaller than JPG?

Usually, often by 25–35% at similar quality, though results vary by image content.
