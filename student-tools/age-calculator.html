About this tool

Get an exact age — not just years, but months and days too — from any birth date, calculated as of today or any date you choose.

How it works

  1. Enter a date of birth.
  2. Optionally change the 'as of' date from today.
  3. Read the exact age in years, months and days.

Frequently asked questions

Can I calculate age as of a future date?

Yes — set the 'as of' date to any future date to see how old someone will be.

Does it account for leap years?

Yes — the calculation uses actual calendar dates, so leap years are handled correctly.
