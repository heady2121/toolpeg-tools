About this tool

Add each course you're taking with its credit hours and letter grade to see your GPA for the term update instantly, on a standard 4.0 or 4.3 scale.

How it works

  1. Add a row for each course.
  2. Set its credit hours and letter grade.
  3. Your GPA updates automatically as you type.

Frequently asked questions

Which grading scale should I choose?

Most US colleges use a 4.0 scale where an A is worth 4.0. Some use a 4.3 scale where an A+ is worth 4.3 — check your institution's catalog if you're unsure.

Does this match my school's exact GPA calculation?

It uses the standard grade-point mapping, but some schools weight certain courses (like honors or AP classes) differently — check with your registrar for anything official.
